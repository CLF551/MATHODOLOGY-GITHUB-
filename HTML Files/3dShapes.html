3D Shapes

last time we learn 2D shapes, now we learn 3D shape!

First, let's identify each types of 3D shapes

We identify it from left to right!

Prism, cylinder, pyramid, cone and sphere!

Let's explain how each type of 3D shape works!

Prism: The two faces are same, but the other faces have rectangle faces. According to the picture, triangle faces each other, but have rectangle faces between!

Cylinder: Two circle faces are facing each other, and one round face is connecting them!

Pyramid: The base face has it's own face, but the other faces on the sides have triangle faces!

Cone: A circle base face, with a curved face with one pointy end on top!

Sphere: A complete round face, there's a lot of spheres with ball like shapes such as the balls used in sports!
