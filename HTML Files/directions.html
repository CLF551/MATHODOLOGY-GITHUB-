Directions

Here we will learn directions, where we will use positions and turning!

Here we can see the 4 basic directions, North, East, South, West!

Now, what if we need to turn into another direction? Let's take an example!

As you can see, the person is facing north right now!

If he turns right or in another term clockwise 90 degree, he faces East!

If he turns left or in the term counter clockwise 90 degree, he faces West!
