Time

Here we are learning the concept of time! Bring out the clocks!

Here is the example of reading the clock. We see the longer hand is the minute, the short one as the hour!

If we were to read this, it will be called 3 o'clock with minute hand at 12 exact and hour hand at 3

What if the minute hand is in a different place?

We won't go too deep, so let's stick with two more examples.

Here we can see the clock on the left, it reads 5 o'clock similar to the first example.

Now the clock on the right is different, this time, we have minute hand at the 6 and hour hand between 9 and 10. We call this half past 9.
