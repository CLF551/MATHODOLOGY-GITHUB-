Date

Here, we learn about the use of date from our time and calender materials!

Here, there are 7 months which have 31 days. January, March, May, July, August, October and December.

There are 4 months which have just 30 days, April, June, September and November.

There's one month which has just 28 days is February. But on leap years, it has 29 days.

Every 4 years, there's a leap year which makes it 366 days. Let's say 2000 is a leap year, 2004 is it's next leap year, the years between are the common years!

With that in mind, let's have an example!

We are in 31st of March, after 31 days, what is the date now?

Here we are in the end of march and april has only 30 days, count off the 30 days and the date right now is 1st of May!
