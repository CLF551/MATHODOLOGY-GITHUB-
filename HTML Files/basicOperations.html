Basic Operations

We now start with some basic operations. With the two most common concepts, addition and subtraction.

Addition

For addition, you need to know the concept of adding numbers using the two symbols. The plus sign "+" and the equal sign "=".

Here I give you an example.
5 + 2 = 7, here we count to 5 and then we add 2 more to get 7.
We can even put like this: Ben has 5 apples, I give him 2 more apples how many apples does he have? Answer: Ben has 7 apples.


Subtraction

For subtraction, we use the term of taking away numbers using similar signs, this time, you use the minus sign "-" and the same equal sign "="

Another example
6 - 3 = 3, here we count to 6 and then we take away 3 to get 3
We can even put it like this: Amy has 6 pears, 3 were taken away. How many pears does she have left? Answer: Amy has 3 pears left.
