Subtraction

Here, we learn more about the use of subtraction

Here as you can see, this is subtracting a number, this time there is a special case of borrowing a number.

Since 2 is not enough to take away from 8, so we borrow a 1 from the 6 to make 12 - 8, to make it as 4. Then, simply do 5 - 2 to make 3 and we get 34!

What if we have to subtract more than one time? Let's make an example here!

20 - 6 - 3 = 14 - 3 = 11, based on this, we are doing this one step at a time.
