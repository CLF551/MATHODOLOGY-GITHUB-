Calender

Here we will learn the concept of the calender!

First, we will learn the names of the seven days in a week!

As it follows: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

Got the days down? Let's move on to the names of the 12 months in a year!

As it follows: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December.

In the summary, every week has 7 days and each month has about 4 weeks! Each year has about 12 months!

Each year has 365 days in total, so each months have about 2 or 3 days extra per month. Except February which rarely has 1 extra day.

There are two types of years, common year and leap year. Common year has 365 days, leap year has 366 days.
Leap year's February always have 29 days.
