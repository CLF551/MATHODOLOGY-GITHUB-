2D Shapes

Here we learn the concept of 2D shapes

From left to right the shapes are named!

Circle, triangle, square, pentagon, hexagon

Now, I explain the basic of each shape shown.

Circle: One curved line connecting, a coin is shaped like a circle!

Triangle: Has 3 sides and 3 corners!

Square: Has 4 sides and 4 corners!

Pentagon: Has 5 sides and 5 corners!

Hexagon: Has 6 sides and 6 corners!

I would suggest take some time to draw the shapes to understand better!
