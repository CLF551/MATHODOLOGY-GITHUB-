Shapes (II)

Here, we learn more about the properties of shapes!

Here, we learn the properties of perimeter and area of the rectangle!

Perimeter of rectangle is to add up all sides and according to the example, we add 4 + 4 + 8 + 8 = 24 cm.

For the area, we multiply the length and width of the rectangle to the example, we do 4 x 8 = 32 cm^2.


This is just one example, what about the triangle?

Now here, we learn the similar pattern for triangle.

Perimeter stays the same, just add up all the sides and we get 5 + 6 + 8 = 19 cm.

However, area is different, so we multiply the width and height then divide by 2. In the way of (5 x 6)/2 = 15 cm^2.
