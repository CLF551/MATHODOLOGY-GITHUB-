Angles

Here we are learning the concept of angles!

Here you can see we have three types of angles. From left to right, we have the acute angle, the right angle and the obtuse angle!

Acute angle has always less than 90 degrees, right angle always have exactly 90 degree and obtuse angle has more than 90 degree but less than 180 degree!

You also need to know the perpendicular term. This is where two straight lines meet each other to be exactly 90 degrees!
