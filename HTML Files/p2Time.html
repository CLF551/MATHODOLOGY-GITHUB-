Time (II)

Here, we will learn more about time! This one's harder!

Last time, we learn about half past, this time let's learn about the minutes. Each hour has 60 minutes.

Here is an example of showing more on how time works!

How about let's put in into a question then?

John plans to meet his friends at 3:35, but he will be 40 minutes late. When will he arrive?

As for the answer, John arrives at 4:15. For every 60 minutes, an hour is added. After 25 minutes, it's 4:00. And then the 15 minutes add up!

There's also the term: AM and PM. AM describes mainly in the morning, PM describes in the afternoon.

There are 4 types of tells in time. Morning, Noon, Afternoon and midnight!

We describe morning about 6am to 12 noon, Noon is exactly 12pm, afternoon between 12pm and 6pm, midnight at 12am exactly.

Note that each day, has 24 hours!, 12 hours each for AM and PM!
