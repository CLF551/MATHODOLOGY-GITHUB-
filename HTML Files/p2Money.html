Money (II)

Let's learn more about money, last time it's coins, what about notes?

From this picture, we can see 10 dollar note, 20 dollar note, 50 dollar note 100 dollar note, 500 dollar note and the 1000 dollar note!

Done looking? How about some examples?

Jane has two 20 dollar notes, four 50 dollar notes and five 100 dollar notes. How much does she have?

We can simply group them up, (2x20) + (4x50) + (5x100) = 40 + 200 + 500 = 740 dollars

This works similar to coins, this time with more value!
